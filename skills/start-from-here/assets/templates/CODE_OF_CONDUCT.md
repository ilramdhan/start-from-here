# Code of Conduct

{{project_name}} has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its Code of Conduct. All contributors, maintainers, and participants in project spaces (repositories, issues, discussions, chat, events) are expected to follow it.

The full text is available at: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Reporting and enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at **{{enforcement_contact}}**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Enforcement follows the [Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
